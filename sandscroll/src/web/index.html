About the scroll

Each picture is drawn the way a sand artist works on a light table: a palm spreads the sand, fingertips carve light out of it, and trickles of sand draw the dark lines. When a picture is finished, a line of classical poetry is written into the sand and stamped with a red seal, completing the four arts of a Chinese scroll: poem, calligraphy, painting and seal.

The music is composed as it plays, in the five-note modes of Chinese classical music, for guzheng, xiao and guqin. Nothing is pre-recorded, so the stream never repeats.

The programme was made for President Xi Jinping's state visit to the United States, 23–25 September 2026. Its images are shared symbols rather than portraits: the moon both shores see, the Great Wall, the 1784 voyage of the Empress of China, the Golden Gate, the giant pandas Ping Ping and Fu Shuang, a rose and a peony, and a bridge of cranes.
